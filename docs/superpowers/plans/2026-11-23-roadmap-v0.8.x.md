# Roadmap for the rest of v0.8.x: ideas, value and where they land

Owner, 2026-11-23, after looking at another personal-agent app:
- "There's a lot to learn here and I don't think we can fit all those in a cut. But we can plan for it and also make our own spin on it in a way that fits Dom."
- "They can continue to be part of v0.8.x."
- Bring the low-hanging fruit and the high-value items into v0.8.3 and v0.8.4; the rest stays documented here. The owner approved this roadmap the same day and moved Gmail into v0.8.4.

## Dom's spin
These rules hold for every item below.
- **A friend, not a task inbox.** The other app is an assistant waiting for work. Dom is a friend with his own life who notices things. A feed item or idea reads like "saw this, want me to?" in his voice, never like a ticket. He offers a few, not a stream.
- **Owner only.** Feed, ideas, goals, search, the library, the credential store and integrations exist only in the owner DM and the owner dashboard. None of them is visible, named or usable in groups or friends' DMs, and nothing from them goes into another chat without the existing approval card (E52).
- **Local first, cloud as fallback** (north star). Credentials and integration tokens stay on the Pi, encrypted at rest, and never pass through a model.
- **Assistant mode is the owner's choice** (R15). When the owner turns it on, Dom also works as a full-time assistant in the owner DM. He is still Dom, and everything above still holds.
- **The owner approves anything that acts.** Reading can be allowed in advance. Sending, buying, deleting or signing in asks first, unless the owner set that action to Allow.

## What goes where
- **v0.8.3** (see its plan): the studio, the models and providers rework, the small fixes and code highlighting, plus R1 memory export, R2 global search (with Dom's tool) and the R3 Google research (plus the R14 ACP research; the pilot is paused), and the first step of memory across chats.
- **v0.8.4**:
  - R4 browser permissions, then R5 the credential store, which needs a security spec and its own review;
  - R6 goals and R7 feed and ideas;
  - R8 Google Calendar, then R10 Gmail;
  - R9 the library;
  - R13 source-tagged memory;
  - the owner-DM fast lane (owner: v0.8.3 or v0.8.4);
  - "Tune with Dom" in Chats.
- **Later in v0.8.x**: R10b, R11, R15 assistant mode (once R6, R7, R8 and R10 are in).
- **Not planned**: R12.

Order inside v0.8.4: R4 before R5 (permissions are the frame the credential store plugs into), and R3's findings before R8.
