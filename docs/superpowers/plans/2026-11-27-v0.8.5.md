# v0.8.5: Dom gets hands, a world of his own, and a window into how he thinks

North star (owner, 2026-11-22): the ultimate personality-driven agent on a local stack, with the cloud as fallback.

v0.8.4 made Dom aware of his own life. v0.8.5 gives him:
- **Hands:** desktop browser permissions, saved logins, Google Calendar and Gmail, each behind approval cards.
- **A world:** a slow-changing life and real places, so his stories and photos stay coherent.
- **A window for the owner:** chat with Dom from the dashboard, with a full debug tracer.
- **A start on assistant mode.**

Baseline:
- the v0.8.4 plan and its "Deferred to v0.8.5" list;
- the roadmap (R4, R5, R8, R10, R11, R15);
- the Google research note `docs/superpowers/notes/v0.8.3-google-integrations.md`;
- the owner decisions here.

The owner plans to finish v0.8.4 and v0.8.5 before anyone else sees Dom.

## Workstreams

### W1 Dom's world (E106)
- **A life sheet:** `memory/WORLD.md`, owned by Dom and editable by the owner.
  - Where he lives (area, home type) and where he works or studies, his commute, and his regular spots (lunch places, a gym, a park).
  - His routines by weekday and weekend, and his current projects.
  - It changes slowly: the memory pass or the nightly reflection may propose a change, and a change applies at most weekly, with a reason. The owner's hand edits are pinned, as in v0.8.4.
- **Places:** entity notes for real places (from W2B's graph) with a short description.
  - Optionally, a reference photo the owner adds on the dashboard, or one Dom picked from search with the owner's approval.
  - Photos and scene stills of Dom at a known place use its reference photo as context when the image model accepts a reference.
- **The prompt:** a compact "your world" block in the turn note (after the window, not in the shared prefix), with today's routine slot (for example "Thursday evening: usually gym") and variety hints, so Dom doesn't repeat himself (the cai-fan problem). Groups and friends' DMs see only what the R13 rules allow.
- **Dashboard:** a World page or Memory section with the sheet, places and reference photos.

### W2 Chat in the dashboard, with the debug tracer (R11, E102)
- **Chat:** talk to Dom from the dashboard. It is the same conversation as the owner DM, not a new chat.
  - Messages typed there are recorded as owner-DM messages that came from the dashboard, and Dom's replies go to both Telegram and the dashboard.
  - Media in both directions, plus a typing state and a streamed bubble view.
  - A side panel with his profile, approvals waiting, and what he's working on (desktop tasks, own work).
- **Tracer toggle:** flips the conversation into the full audit, per turn:
  1. Arrival and triage: the Jev scores and decisions, the attention-loop reason, the queue wait, presence and away state.
  2. The exact prompt: the system message, the notes blocks, the chat window XML, the turn note, the tools offered (names and full schemas), and native media parts. It shows which parts hit the cache (the prompt fingerprints and cached tokens).
  3. Every model call: route, provider and model, fallbacks, the full request and response JSON, reasoning where the model returns it, tokens, cached tokens, cost and timing.
  4. Every tool call: arguments, result, errors (for example "image is not a tool"), and the durations.
  5. Output checks: leak scan scores, cutoffs and holds, the private-text check, and the outcomes of the media look-before-send.
  6. Delivery: each bubble as sent, splits, edits, deletes, reactions, and studio jobs with their provenance.
  7. After the turn: memory-pass ops, tags, pins, the knowledge-index refresh, and noticings or goal updates.
- **Storage:**
  - A trace store with a retention setting (default 7 days) and a size cap. Friends' and groups' turns are full too (E102).
  - Secrets (API keys, tokens, `.env` values, Telegram bot token) are redacted before storage.
  - Export as JSON per turn or per time range. The Turn log links each row to its trace.
- **Cost:** recording must not slow replies. Write traces after the turn, off the reply path, bounded in memory.

### W3 Desktop permissions, saved logins and secure secrets (R4, R5, E100, E101)
- **A security spec first:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-credentials-and-permissions.md`. It covers the threat model, the key handling, what never touches a model or the sandbox API's traced requests, and the approval UX. Implementation follows it.
- **R4 permissions:** each desktop action kind gets Ask, Allow or Never: visiting an unfamiliar site, filling a saved login, downloading, uploading, and background work.
  - Per-site allow lists, plus an approvals history on the dashboard.
  - Approval cards in the owner DM with Reject, Allow once and Allow always (E100).
- **R5 saved logins:**
  - The owner saves site logins on the dashboard. They are encrypted at rest with a key in `.env`, and entries show masked.
  - The desktop browser fills them through the sandbox without the password passing through a model or traced requests. Follow the post-v0.8.0 notes about page/type tracing.
  - Auto password mode in the viewer, when the focused field is a password field.
- **E101 secure secret request:** an owner-DM tool `request_secret` for Dom. He gives a label and a reason; Dom gets a one-time dashboard form link; the value is stored and Dom learns only "set". It is owner-DM only.

### W4 Google Calendar and Gmail (R8, R10, E103)
- **Connect flow** in Settings → Services: Connect Google, with consent covering the three scopes.
  - The callback at `/api/integrations/google/callback` checks the state (and PKCE if supported) and works only for the owner's dashboard session.
  - Tokens are encrypted at rest. Revoke and disconnect are supported.
  - An expired or revoked token produces one owner-DM notice. Each scope gets its own switch.
- **Calendar:**
  - Dom reads upcoming events (owner DM only) for awareness in turns.
  - Adding, moving or deleting an event always goes through an approval card, Allow once or Always per kind.
  - Events feed reminders and goals.
- **Gmail:**
  - Read and search mail (owner DM only). Mail content goes to the chat model only in the owner DM, and is treated as untrusted text.
  - Drafts are created only through a card. Sending is never automatic.
- **Privacy:** nothing from Calendar or Gmail reaches groups or friends' DMs, and code filters this. The docs cover what goes to which model.

### W5 Owner-DM fast lane (D15)
A second lane for the owner DM and urgent items next to the main lane, so the owner's DM and approval cards stay responsive while long work runs elsewhere. The lanes share the prompt prefix but never write to one transcript. The Turn log shows each turn's lane and queue wait.

### W6 Assistant mode (R15), the core
- **An owner-set switch** in the owner DM: Dom keeps the owner's day in view.
  - A morning plan (calendar, reminders, goals and weather) and an evening wrap-up, each optional.
  - He follows through on what he took on, and checks in on reminders and goals without being asked.
  - Off, he is the friend he is today. It never leaks into groups or friends' DMs.
- **Appointment booking**, behind its own switch, only if time allows:
  - Dom finds free slots, offers a few to a friend in their DM (only the times, never what else is in the calendar), and agrees one.
  - The booking is an approval card unless set to Allow.

### W7 Carry-overs from v0.8.4
- Video notes barely move the face: try FlashHead's motion settings, or another lip-sync model, on the studio Mac.
- Media promised "in a sec" without being sent: treat a promise in a bubble the same way as text-written media, and nudge the model.
- Per-chat media used and left for chats other than the owner DM.
- More "world now" context: public holidays, plus local events or news headlines if a free source fits.
- Chat titles for chats the bot has never seen.
